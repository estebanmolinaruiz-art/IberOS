IberOS

IberOS is an independent open research project created by Esteban Molina Ruiz for reproducible, computationally assisted analysis of ancient Iberian inscriptions.

The project combines archaeological documentation, published readings, provenance controls and computational methods. It explicitly separates evidence, inference, hypothesis and estimated translation.

IberOS does not claim to have deciphered the Iberian language. Its purpose is to make analytical procedures, limitations and negative results auditable and reproducible.
