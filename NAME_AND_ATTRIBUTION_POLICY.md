# IberOS Name and Attribution Policy

**IberOS** identifies the official project created and maintained by **Esteban Molina Ruiz**.

Descriptive uses such as “generated with IberOS”, “compatible with IberOS” or “based on IberOS” are encouraged.

Modified distributions should be identified as forks, derivatives, unofficial releases or projects based on IberOS. They should not present themselves simply as the official IberOS distribution without authorization.

This naming policy helps preserve project identity and scientific traceability. It is not, by itself, a registered trademark.
