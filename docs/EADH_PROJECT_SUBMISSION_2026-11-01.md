## Summary (max. 100 words)
IberOS is an independent open research project created by Esteban Molina Ruiz for reproducible, computationally assisted analysis of ancient Iberian inscriptions. It combines archaeological documentation, published epigraphic readings, provenance controls and computational methods. The workflow explicitly separates evidence, inference, hypothesis and estimated translation, while preserving uncertainty, documentary dependence and negative results. IberOS does not claim to have deciphered the Iberian language. Its public resources include a curated corpus, research records, reproducible methods, open-source software, DOI-backed publications and an experimental research environment for digital epigraphy, archaeology and Digital Humanities.
